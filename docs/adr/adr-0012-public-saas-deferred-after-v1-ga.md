# ADR-0012: Public SaaS deferred after self-host v1 GA

- **Status:** Proposed (2026-08-02) — owner approval pending for implementation authorization
- **Plan task:** G0.7
- **Date:** 2026-08-02

## Context

Public signup, billing, and noisy-neighbor controls are large workstreams (S+).

## Decision

Public multi-tenant SaaS is **after R3** self-host Full Workspace v1 GA (Phase S+). Marketing must not claim SaaS readiness at v1 GA.

## Consequences

S+.* tasks are non-blocking for R3. Compose/Helm self-host paths are the v1 GA deploy targets.

## Reversal

Pull SaaS into v1 only with explicit owner re-scope and expanded evidence.
